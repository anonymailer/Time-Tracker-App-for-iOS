My Work Hours App for IOS

Track your working hours. Calculate your earnings. Stay organised.

My Work Hours is a simple and convenient iOS application designed to help individuals record their daily working hours, calculate earnings, manage work schedules, and generate detailed monthly reports.

Whether you work full-time, part-time, freelance, or on an hourly basis, My Work Hours helps you keep track of your working time and payments in one place.

Features

1. Calendar & Daily Work Records

* Easy-to-use calendar interface.
* Record working hours for individual days.
* Enter starting and finishing times.
* Set your hourly payment rate.
* Add personal notes to each working day.
* Automatically calculate daily working hours.
* Automatically calculate daily earnings.
* View total working hours.

2. Working Hours Templates

Save your regular working schedules to avoid entering the same information repeatedly.

Each template includes:

* Template name.
* Starting time (From).
* Finishing time (To).
* Hourly payment rate.
* Notes.
* Option to set a default template.

Apply saved templates to your daily records whenever needed.

3. Monthly Reports

View detailed monthly working reports.

Navigate between months using the previous and next buttons.

Reports include:

* Date in DD-MM-YYYY format.
* Day of the week.
* Working time.
* Total working hours.
* Daily earnings.
* Personal notes.
* Monthly total working hours.
* Monthly total earnings.

4. Customisable Report Export

Create personalised reports using custom export profiles.

Customisation options include:

* Select which columns to include.
* Choose date ranges.
* Include or exclude monthly totals.
* Select preferred time format.
* Set currency preferences.
* Save multiple export profiles.

Supported export formats:

* PDF
* TXT
* CSV

Export and share your reports whenever you need them.

5. Multiple Profiles

Manage different work profiles and keep your working records organised separately.

6. Personalisation & Settings

Customise the application according to your preferences.

Available settings:

* Light theme.
* Dark theme.
* System theme.
* 12-hour time format.
* 24-hour time format.
* Selectable currencies.
* Application language selection.

Privacy

My Work Hours is designed for personal productivity and work-hour management.

Your working records may contain personal information. They should be stored securely and should not be shared without your permission.
